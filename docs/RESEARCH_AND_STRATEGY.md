## 1. Understand the client

### What we know / are assuming
- GK Botanicals: B2B manufacturer of standardised botanical extracts (moringa, ashwagandha, turmeric/curcumin, bacopa, ginger, boswellia, ginkgo, green tea…).
- Audience: global brand owners, formulators, contract manufacturers, procurement and QA/regulatory teams.
- Goal: show purity, innovation and compliance; generate sample / COA / quote requests.

### Client interview checklist (needs a real conversation)
| Topic | Questions |
|---|---|
| Business | Year founded, location(s), ownership, team size. Monthly capacity (MT)? Own farms / contract farming / third-party sourcing? |
| Products | Full product list with marker compounds and typical specs. Any **branded / clinically-backed ingredients**? Organic lines? Custom/private-label capability? |
| Proof | Which certifications are **actually held** (GMP, ISO 9001/22000, FSSC, organic, Kosher, Halal, FSSAI/DSIR…), issuing body, validity, scope? Audit-readiness? Lab instruments (HPLC/HPTLC/GC/LC-MS)? |
| Markets | Top export regions today and targets; regulatory needs per region (FDA/NDI, EU novel food, TGA…). |
| Buyers | Who signs off — R&D, procurement, QA? Typical MOQ, lead time, payment terms, sample policy? |
| Differentiators | Why do customers choose GK over alternatives? Win/loss stories, repeat-customer share, references (with permission). |
| Pain points | Current website problems, lead quality, what sales has to explain repeatedly. |
| Brand | Logo, colours, tone, existing photography/video, factory access for shoots. |
| Success | Target: samples requested / month, RFQs, newsletter, hiring? Budget and launch date. |

## 2. Research the business

### Category landscape
Botanical-extract suppliers compete on **proof and reliability**, not on look-and-feel. Typical B2B buyer journey:
search (ingredient + "manufacturer/supplier", often with country) → product page with spec → COA/spec-sheet download →
sample request → audit/qualification → PO. The site must serve QA/regulatory readers (data, documents, certificates) as much as marketers.

### Buyer pain points the site should answer
1. "Is this supplier real and audited?" → certificates, factory, capacity, lab, team.
2. "Does the material meet my spec?" → marker %, method (HPLC…), COA sample, heavy metals/pesticide/micro limits.
3. "Can they supply consistently and traceably?" → sourcing, batch traceability, capacity, lead times.
4. "Can they customise?" → custom extracts, ratios, private label, organic.
5. "How do I get a sample/quote fast?" → short form, named contact, response-time promise.

## 4. Where GK Botanicals can win
1. **Proof first, theatre second:** keep the cinematic hero, but put verifiable proof (certificates, capacity, lab methods, COA sample) one scroll away.
2. **Make documents easy:** per-product spec sheet + sample COA downloads, 2-field sample request.
3. **Explain the science visibly:** the extraction-technology section (aqueous / ethanol / scCO₂) is a differentiator — pair it with which products use which method.
4. **Own the long-tail search:** product-by-intent and country pages (without keyword stuffing; real content per page).
5. **Credibility artefacts:** factory/lab photography, team, timeline, customer logos/testimonials with permission.
